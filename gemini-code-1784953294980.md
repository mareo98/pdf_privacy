# Privacy Policy for Qabooly

**Last Updated:** July 25, 2026

**Qabooly – Iraqi University Guide** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, storage, and usage when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Account Registration

* **No Account Required:** Qabooly does **not** require any user registration, sign-in, or account creation.
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, or request any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, government identity, or precise GPS location.

---

### 2. Local Device Storage

To enhance your experience, the application may save certain non-personal preferences locally on your device's internal storage, including:
* Saved favorite universities, colleges, and departments.
* Custom-built admission preference lists or notes.
* App UI configurations and theme settings.

> **Note:** All locally stored data remains entirely on your device and is never transmitted, synced, or uploaded to any external server or third party.

---

### 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app utilizes **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), and anonymous network data to serve ads based on general user interactions.

* You can review how Google handles data in advertising by visiting the [Google Privacy & Terms Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* You can manage or reset your device advertising ID via your Android system settings (**Settings > Privacy > Ads**).

---

### 4. Data Security

Because we do not host user accounts or transmit personal information to external servers, your stored preferences remain isolated on your local device under standard operating system security protections. All third-party advertising interactions are transmitted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Qabooly is a general audience educational tool intended for students and academic applicants. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be effective immediately upon publishing the revised policy within the app or on its Google Play Store listing.
