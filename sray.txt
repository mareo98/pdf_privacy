# Privacy Policy for SARAY

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2026

**SARAY – سراي** ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, storage, and usage when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Account Registration

* **Account Requirements:** SARAY may require user or vendor account registration to manage orders, multi-vendor profiles, or shopping preferences.
* **Personal Data Collected:** We may collect necessary account information (such as name, email address, phone number, and delivery address) solely to facilitate shopping, store management, and order fulfillment processes within the application. We do not collect precise GPS location tracking or government identity documents unless explicitly required for verified vendor accounts.

---

### 2. Local Device Storage and Data Syncing

To enhance your shopping and browsing experience, the application may save certain preferences locally on your device's internal storage, including:
* Saved favorite products, stores, and shopping cart items.
* Custom-built search filters, lists, or local preferences.
* App UI configurations and theme settings.

> **Note:** Locally stored data remains primarily on your device. Necessary transactional data required for order processing and multi-vendor communication is securely processed and synced through our secure servers.

---

### 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app may utilize **Google AdMob** and other trusted third-party services to display advertisements or handle app functionalities. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), and anonymous network data to serve ads based on general user interactions.

* You can review how Google handles data in advertising by visiting the [Google Privacy & Terms Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* You can manage or reset your device advertising ID via your Android system settings (**Settings > Privacy > Ads**).

---

### 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information and account credentials against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. All communications and data transmissions between the app and external servers are conducted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

SARAY is a general audience e-commerce and marketplace tool intended for general users and shoppers. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be effective immediately upon publishing the revised policy within the app or on its Google Play Store listing.
